ESTABLISHED 1924

The Flourished Hearth

Our journey began with a simple wild yeast starter and a dream to bring the honest taste of slow-fermented bread to our community. Every loaf is a three-day labor of love, honoring traditions passed through generations.

We source our heritage grains from local organic farms, milling them stone-cold to preserve every nutrient and flavor profile. It's not just bread; it's a testament to patience, heat, and the flourished hearth.
